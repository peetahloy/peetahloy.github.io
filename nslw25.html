National School Lunch Week 2025

Project Overview

National School Lunch Week (NSLW) is a USDA initiative celebrating healthy school meals. In partnership with this campaign, the Texas Department of Agriculture (TDA) created a collection of print-ready promotional materials, including banners, menu displays, activity books, coloring pages, and more, to help Texas ISDs engage students statewide. All assets were designed for easy, low-cost printing, and contest-winning schools received custom décor kits tailored to their campuses.

Project Timeline: 2 months

Stakeholders:

  • Directors and Assistant Directors of TDA
  • Vice Commissioner and Commissioner of TDA
  • Texas ISDs across the state

My Role

Graphic Design & Outreach Intern

  • Conceptualized and designed promotional assets for the NSLW campaign.
  • Collaborated with marketing and design teams to create print-ready materials for statewide distribution.
  • Prepared and produced custom printed materials for contest winners.

Worked closely with TDA’s Food and Nutrition Program Advancement team, including Marlaina Edwards (Marketing and Outreach Senior) and Santana Lopez (Graphic Design Specialist), under the supervision of Alonzo Villarreal (Assistant Director of Program Advancement).

All rights belong to the Texas Department of Agriculture. Work shown for portfolio purposes only.
